# DuoChat — Chat & Play Together

DuoChat is a simple peer to peer chat app made for two people. You create a room, share the code with your friend, and start chatting instantly. No sign up, no backend, no database needed.

This app works directly in the browser. Just open the file and it works.

## What You Can Do

You can chat in real time with your friend. Messages appear instantly with time and your chosen avatar.

You can see when your friend is typing.

You can react to messages with emojis like heart, laugh, fire and more. When you react, the emoji bursts on the screen.

You can send emoji bursts to make the chat more fun.

You can play with fun actions like rolling a dice, flipping a coin, sending a nudge to shake your friend's screen, sending confetti, and getting random questions to talk about.

You can draw together on a shared board. Whatever you draw, your friend sees it live. You can choose different colors and brush sizes.

You can play games together inside the chat. There is Tic Tac Toe and Rock Paper Scissors with live score.

The design is modern glass style with blur and soft colors. It works well on phone and computer. You can switch between dark and light theme and turn sound on or off.

When you create a room, you get a short six character code. You can share the code or share a link. Your friend can join by entering the code.

## How It Works In Simple Words

One person creates a room and gets a code. The other person joins with that code. Both browsers connect directly to each other without going through a server for messages. This makes it fast and private.

## How To Use

First open the app in your browser. Enter your name and choose an avatar you like.

Then click create room. You will see a code and a waiting screen.

Share the code or the invite link with your friend.

Your friend opens the app, enters their name, clicks join room and types the code.

Once connected, you can start chatting, drawing, and playing.

## Privacy

DuoChat does not save any messages or data. Everything happens directly between the two browsers. When you both leave, the room is gone. Only two people can be in one room at a time.
